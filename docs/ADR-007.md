# ADR-007: Training Architecture & Intelligence Strategy

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-05-25

## Context
The Training Tracker requires a flexible hierarchy for multi-week plans and a way to handle distinct exercise types (Strength vs. Cardio). Additionally, the system needs an extensible "Virtual Trainer" to provide progression advice.

## Decision
1.  **Hierarchical Plan Model**:
    - `TrainingPlan`: The parent entity representing a time-bound commitment (e.g., "8 Weeks Hypertrophy").
    - `TrainingRoutine`: Reusable templates within a plan (e.g., "Day A: Upper Body").
    - `TrainingSession`: A concrete instance of a workout, optionally linked to a `TrainingPlan` and `TrainingRoutine`.
2.  **Entity-Level Separation of Logging**:
    - Use `TrainingSet` exclusively for **Strength** exercises (Reps, Weight).
    - Introduce `CardioRecord` for **Cardio** exercises (Time, Distance, Pace).
    - Both are linked to a shared `TrainingExercise` anchor in the session.
3.  **Intelligence Strategy Pattern**:
    - The "Virtual Trainer" logic is decoupled into strategies.
    - `TrainingSettings` stores the user's preferred strategy (`weight-first` vs `reps-first`).
    - The backend calculates suggestions by comparing the `last_performance` with the chosen strategy.
4.  **Real-time UI**:
    - The active session uses a standalone state service to manage the **Stopwatch** and "Live Workout" buffer before persistence.

## Consequences
- **Pros**: 
  - Cleaner data model by separating Reps/Weight from Time/Distance.
  - Extensible Trainer logic (can easily add AI-driven strategies later).
  - High flexibility for users who mix plan-based and free workouts.
- **Cons**: 
  - More complex frontend forms (dynamic component loading for Strength vs Cardio).
  - Requires migration/refactor of existing `TrainingPlanTemplate`.
